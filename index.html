Beaumont Barbers is a return to what barbering should be — unhurried, intentional, and grounded in craft. No rushed chairs, no rotating cast of stylists. Just honest work with natural products and a steady hand.

We use organic, vegan-friendly grooming products, source our towels from local suppliers, and take the time to understand what you want — not what's trending. Every cut is a conversation.

"Good hair doesn't need chemicals. It needs care, craft, and a little patience."
— Mark Beaumont, Founder
